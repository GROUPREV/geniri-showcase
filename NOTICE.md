## What is published

- The written explanation: the README, the architecture, the decision records and
  the programme record.
- Two short excerpts of real code, each with a comment explaining what it does.

The excerpts are taken from the production repository. They are trimmed to the
parts that carry the point and are otherwise unedited, so what is shown is the
code that runs, not an illustration of it.

## What is deliberately withheld

- **The application source.** No full source tree, no package internals, no
  migrations or seeds.
- **The instruction layer.** How prompts are constructed, how models are chosen
  for a task, how output quality is measured per credit, and the benchmarking that
  feeds those choices. This is the core of the product and the part the company
  intends to keep.
- **Provider relationships.** Commercial terms, volumes and unpublished
  agreements with model providers.
- **Commercial detail.** Unpublished pricing, margins, customer names and
  customer material.
- **Administrative internals.** Anything that would help someone misuse the
  admin surface.
- **Credentials and personal data.** None appear here. Screenshots, when added,
  will use demo accounts and invented data.
