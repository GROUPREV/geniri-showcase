## What was already here

Geniri existed before the programme. The platform, its design language and its
commercial model were built by the same team over the preceding year, and the
programme added to them rather than starting from a blank page. Anyone reading
this repository should hold both facts at once: the four weeks are represented by
the release work in [docs/program.md](docs/program.md), and the platform around it
is the context that work happened in.
